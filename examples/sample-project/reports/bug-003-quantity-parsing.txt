Subject: bad quantity in an order file becomes None and the order is dropped silently

When a customer uploads an order with a non-numeric quantity, `parse_quantity`
returns None instead of raising, so the row is dropped and the customer is
charged for less than they ordered. Yesterday we shipped 40 units of the wrong
thing because of this.
